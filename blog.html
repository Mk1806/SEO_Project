SEO Tips for Better Visibility

1. Use the Right Keywords

Research keywords your audience is searching for and include them naturally in your content.

2. Optimize Meta Tags

Ensure every page has a clear title and description to improve search engine indexing.

3. Improve Readability

Break content into headings and short paragraphs to enhance user experience.

4. Internal Linking

Link pages within your website to improve navigation and SEO structure.
